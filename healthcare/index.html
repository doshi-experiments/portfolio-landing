Work · Case 03 of 03

Redesigning benefits for 12 million Kaiser members.

The legacy benefits portal wasn’t built to scale with a growing catalogue of benefits and plan structures, and it confused members enough to drive a large number of calls. The answer was a systems-driven redesign of how members understand costs and coverage.

Spec
Role
Lead UX Designer
Team
2 designers, 1 content designer
Duration
14 months, ongoing
Sector
Healthcare and benefits, B2C
Stakeholders
6 cross-functional teams

Outcomes

What the redesign has done so far.

  • +27%

    Task completion, year over year (N = 20k sessions).

  • 6+

    Net-new features shipped, including an interactive financial tracker for deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums.

  • 3

    Upcoming releases guided by a North-Star vision and roadmap that senior leadership adopted.

Complexity at a glance

Why the old portal couldn’t simply be reskinned.

  1. 1

    Two regulatory domains

    Healthcare and financial, at the same time.

  2. 2

    Hundreds of plan configurations

    Across 6 regions and multiple business units.

  3. 3

    Patterns that don’t scale

    Existing patterns that were hard to extend.

How we got there

Strategy and research first, then design that ships in two-week sprints.

Strategy and research

  • Systems mapping of how benefits data originates, flows and scales across regions.
  • Journey mapping of members’ care pathways and mental models across life stages.
  • Design sprints, competitive analysis and cross-functional workshops to drive alignment.

Design execution

  • A scalable solution covering 200+ plan permutations and future benefit types.
  • An in-product financial tracker showing real-time spend against coverage, across 60+ configurations.
  • Responsive, WCAG AA flows shipped in lockstep with two-week Agile sprints.
