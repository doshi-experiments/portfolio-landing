Redefining the travel insurance claims process with generative AI.

Travel insurance claims in the US are complex, slow and paperwork-heavy, which leads to delays, denials and dissatisfaction. The goal: upload your documents, and let the app work out the claim, file it, and keep you posted.

Two sides of a claim

The person filing it, and the person deciding it.

S
SarahSmall business owner and travel blogger, new customer

“I have very little time and a whole lot of work I’m juggling.”

Wants comprehensive coverage, to manage policies and claims on the go, and quick support. Her journey starts with a broken leg on vacation.

C
ClayClaims adjudicator, 15+ years in travel insurance

“My job is to make sure every claim is evaluated fairly and accurately.”

Wants accurate, efficient processing, transparent communication, and technology that cuts processing time.

The journey, with genAI

Upload the documents, the app works out the claim type, you review it, and the app keeps you updated.

SubmissionOngoing checks for accuracy and completeness.
