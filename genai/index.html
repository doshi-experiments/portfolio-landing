Redefining the travel insurance claims process using genAI

Overview

Travel insurance claims filing in the U.S. is often a complex, time-consuming process involving extensive paperwork, documentation, and bureaucratic hurdles. Travelers frequently face challenges in navigating these processes, leading to delays, claim denials, and overall dissatisfaction. There is a need for an efficient, streamlined solution that simplifies the claims filing experience, reduces errors, and accelerates processing times.

What Matters to Our Users

Simplicity and Clarity

Travelers need insurance processes that are easy to understand, desiring intuitive interfaces and clear communication that eliminate complex jargon.

Speed and Efficiency

Customers demand rapid claim processing and quick resolutions, expecting fast updates and efficient handling to minimize disruptions.

Transparency

Users seek tailored solutions and transparent processes, valuing personalized advice and clear explanations to build trust and satisfaction.

Some of our biggest opportunities

GenAI automates claims processing, personalizes interactions, and delivers real-time updates for a seamless user experience.

Reduce Costs

By automating routine tasks for the claims team, GenAI reduces operational costs, allowing for more strategic resource allocation, enabling the team to focus on more complex tasks.

Data-Driven Insights

What could the journey look like with genAI?

Document Upload

Users upload necessary documents, which are digitally scanned for instant processing.

  • NLP
  • OCR Technology
  • Immediate Capture
  • Document Library

Submission

Claims are submitted with ongoing checks for accuracy and completeness.

  • Real-Time Validation
  • Consistency Checks
  • Error Notification
  • Instant Submission

Claim Identification

The system automatically classifies the claim type based on analyzed data.

  • AI Analysis
  • Auto-Classification
  • Policy Cross-Reference
  • Claim Categorization

Proactive Status

Users receive automatic notifications about claim progress and next steps.

  • Push Notifications
  • Real-Time Updates
  • User Alerts
  • Milestone Tracking

In lesser words, the user uploads documents, the app figures out what claim they're trying to file based on the documents, let's the user review and gives them updates.

Key Features

Form Auto-Fill

GenAI completes forms with extracted data, highlighting missing details for user review.

Simplified Resolution Explainers

Provides clear, AI-generated explanations of claim outcomes and next steps.

Continuous Validation with Alerts

Real-time GenAI checks ensure data integrity and alert users to inconsistencies.

AI Claim Profiling

Auto-categorizes claims by cross- referencing extracted data with policy details.

Smart Document Intake

A GenAI-driven OCR and NLP for accurate data extraction and claim type identification.

Interactive AI Q&A:

Adaptive AI chatbot guides users with tailored questions to fill information gaps.

Essentially, a mix of optical character recognition (OCR), LLMs, and ML-driven claim profiling makes the experience feel like magic.
